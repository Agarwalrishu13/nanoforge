Once upon a time there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to run in the park. Every day Tim would run to the pond and look at the ducks. The ducks would swim and Tim would laugh. One day Tim saw a big frog by the pond. The frog was green and had big eyes. Tim said hello to the frog. The frog jumped into the water and Tim laughed even more. Tim ran home to tell his mom about the frog. His mom smiled and gave Tim a snack. Tim was happy.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sara. Sara had a red ball. She took the ball to the yard and threw it up high. The ball went over the fence and landed in the grass next door. Sara was sad. She knocked on the door and a nice old man opened it. The old man gave Sara the ball back and smiled. Sara said thank you and ran home. She played with her red ball all day long.

Once upon a time there was a dog named Max. Max loved to dig holes in the sand. One day Max dug a very deep hole and found a shiny bone. Max took the bone to his best friend, a cat named Luna. Luna did not want the bone. Luna wanted to nap in the sun. So Max buried the bone by the tree and napped next to Luna. The two friends slept in the warm sun all afternoon.

Once upon a time there was a cat named Luna. Luna liked to sit on the windowsill and watch the birds. The birds flew from tree to tree and Luna would watch them all day. One day a little bird landed right by the window. Luna was very still. The bird sang a song and then flew away. Luna purred and went back to sleep in the sun.

Once upon a time there was a bear named Ben. Ben lived in a big forest. Every morning Ben would eat berries for breakfast. Ben loved berries more than anything. One day the berries were all gone. Ben walked far to find more berries. He found a whole hill of berry bushes. Ben ate and ate until his tummy was full. Then Ben walked home and went to sleep.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Leo. Leo wanted to learn to fly a kite. His dad took him to the hill where the wind was strong. The kite went up and up into the sky. Leo held the string tight and laughed. The kite danced in the wind like a bird. When the sun went down Leo and his dad walked home. Leo asked to fly the kite again the next day.

Once upon a time there was a bunny named Pip. Pip lived in a cozy burrow under a big oak tree. Every night Pip would hop out to look at the moon. The moon was bright and round. One night Pip saw a shooting star. Pip made a wish for more carrots. The next morning there was a whole garden of carrots near the burrow. Pip was the happiest bunny in the meadow.

Once upon a time there was a fish named Bubbles. Bubbles lived in a clear blue pond. Bubbles swam with all the other fish every day. One day Bubbles found a pretty shiny stone at the bottom of the pond. All the fish came to see it. The shiny stone lit up the whole pond at night. Bubbles shared the stone with everyone and the pond glowed like the stars.

Once upon a time there was a little train named Chug. Chug carried apples from the farm to the town. Every day Chug went click clack down the track. One rainy day a big tree fell on the track. Chug could not get through. The farmers came with ropes and pulled the tree away. Chug tooted a happy whistle and carried the apples to town. The whole town had apple pie that night.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Mia. Mia loved to paint pictures. She painted the sun, the trees, and her dog Spot. One day Mia painted a picture of a rainbow. When she was done, a real rainbow appeared in the sky. Her mom said the rainbow wanted to say hello. Mia waved at the rainbow and waved at the sun. Then she painted one more picture before dinner.

Once upon a time there was an owl named Otto. Otto slept all day and woke up at night. At night the forest was quiet and full of stars. Otto would fly from tree to tree and say who who to his friends. The fireflies blinked like tiny lanterns. Otto loved the night more than anything. When the sun came up Otto tucked his head under his wing and dreamed of stars.

Once upon a time there was a hedgehog named Hazel. Hazel had tiny feet and a big heart. She liked to collect leaves in the fall. Red leaves, gold leaves, and orange leaves. Hazel made a big pile of leaves and jumped right in. The leaves flew everywhere like confetti. Her friends the mice came to jump too. They played until the moon came up and then they all went home.

Once upon a time there was a duck named Waddles. Waddles liked to splash in puddles after the rain. Splash splash went Waddles in every puddle on the path. One day the puddles were all dry. Waddles waited and waited for rain. Then one dark cloud came and the rain went pitter patter. Waddles ran out and splashed in the biggest puddle he had ever seen.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Sam. Sam had a wagon full of rocks. Big rocks, small rocks, and rocks with stripes. Sam pulled the wagon to his grandma's house. Grandma said that was a lot of rocks. Sam gave grandma the best striped rock as a gift. Grandma put it on the windowsill where it shined in the sun. Sam and grandma had cookies and milk.

Once upon a time there was a cow named Bluebell. Bluebell lived on a green hill with all the other cows. Every day Bluebell ate grass and watched the clouds go by. The clouds looked like castles and dragons and boats. One day a cloud looked exactly like Bluebell. She mooed at it and the cloud mooed back with thunder. Then it rained and the grass grew greener than ever.

Once upon a time there was a mouse named Milo. Milo was very small but very brave. He lived in a cozy hole in the wall of an old barn. Every night Milo would sneak a crumb of cheese from the barn cat's bowl. The cat never caught him because Milo was too quick. One day Milo left a sunflower seed on the cat's pillow. From then on the cat and the mouse shared the cheese.

Once upon a time there was a pony named Daisy. Daisy had a white mane and spots like snow. She carried children on her back at the fair every spring. The children would pat her nose and feed her apples. One year a little girl was scared to ride. Daisy stood very still and gentle. The girl petted her nose and then rode around the ring with a big smile. Daisy got two apples that day.

Once upon a time there was a squirrel named Nutkin. Nutkin gathered acorns all autumn long. He hid them under logs and in tree holes. Winter came and the snow fell soft and quiet. Nutkin dug up his acorns one by one and had feast after feast. In spring he forgot where some acorns were buried. Those acorns grew into little oak trees. Nutkin had planted a forest without knowing it.

Once upon a time there was a goose named Grace. Grace flew south every winter with her flock. They flew in a big V across the sky. One year a young goose got tired and fell behind. Grace flew beside him and sang a honking song. Slowly the young goose grew strong again. They caught up with the flock just in time to see the warm sea. Grace knew that helping a friend is the best way to fly.

Once upon a time there was a tortoise named Shel. Shel walked everywhere very slowly. The other animals would zoom past him. But Shel always got where he was going. One day the river flooded the meadow and only Shel knew the high path over the hill. He led all the animals up the path step by step. Nobody zoomed past Shel that day. They all walked slow and safe behind him.

Once upon a time there was a puppy named Pepper. Pepper liked to chew on shoes and sticks and sometimes the newspaper. One day Pepper chewed grandpa's favorite slipper. Grandpa was not mad. He gave Pepper a chew toy shaped like a bone. Pepper loved the toy more than any shoe. She slept with it every night and dreamed of sticks and shoes.

Once upon a time there was a robin named Rusty. Rusty built a nest out of mud and twigs on the porch light. Every morning the family watched through the window. Four blue eggs sat in the nest. One by one the eggs cracked and four baby robins popped out. Rusty brought them worms all day long. When the babies learned to fly they filled the whole yard with song.

Once upon a time there was a frog named Hopscotch. Hopscotch lived under a big green leaf by the stream. He sang ribbit ribbit every evening with the other frogs. One evening a little girl sat by the stream and listened. She clapped after every song. Hopscotch puffed up his chest and sang the loudest ribbit of his life. The girl came back every evening to hear the frog choir.

Once upon a time there was a lamb named Cotton. Cotton was white and fluffy like a cloud. She followed the shepherd everywhere and bleated at his shadow. One foggy morning Cotton got lost in the mist. She bleated and bleated. The shepherd heard her tiny voice and came with his lantern. Cotton followed the light all the way home. That night she slept in the warm barn with the lamp burning bright.

Once upon a time there was a raccoon named Bandit. Bandit had a dark mask around his eyes and clever little hands. He could open jars and latches and even the garbage can. But Bandit's favorite thing was washing his food in the creek. Everything he ate got a little bath first. One day he washed a strawberry so long it came apart in the water. Bandit learned that some things are best eaten dry.

Once upon a time there was a firefly named Flicker. Flicker had the brightest light in the whole meadow. Every summer night the fireflies danced over the grass. Flicker liked to spell words with his light. One night he spelled goodnight for all the field mice. The mice looked up and giggled. From then on Flicker ended every night the same way. Goodnight meadow, goodnight moon, goodnight friends.
